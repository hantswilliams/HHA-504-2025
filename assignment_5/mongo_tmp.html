Learning Objectives

By completing this assignment, you will be able to:

  1. Describe MongoDB as a document database and where it fits in modern application architectures.
  2. Compare relational (SQL) and document (NoSQL) data models at a high level.
  3. Recognize when a document model may be more appropriate than a traditional relational schema.
  4. Connect MongoDB concepts to healthcare-relevant data scenarios.
